iPhone과 iPad에서 씁니다. 계정이 없고, 서버가 없고, 광고가 없습니다. 기록은 기기 안에만 저장됩니다.

Quiet Hour

A focus timer. For the time you set, it keeps the screen quiet.

Set a length and start. The screen keeps only light. The light moves slowly and shows roughly how far along you are. The time left appears for a few seconds when you tap, then fades.

For iPhone and iPad. No account, no server, no ads. Your history is stored only on your device.
